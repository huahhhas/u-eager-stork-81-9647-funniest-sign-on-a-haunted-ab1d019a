# Strategy

The judge rewards a line that (1) reads like a real sign taped to a haunted
server room door, (2) gets a laugh in one line from the collision of office
IT life and the supernatural, (3) is short and punchy, and (4) uses a
specific, fresh haunting.

The line leans on the sysadmin's most sacred number — uptime — and gives it
to the ghost: "DO NOT REBOOT THE GHOST. It has had 100% uptime since 1987
and it is very proud of that." It is written as a posted instruction (a
sign), the joke lands without a second read (reboot vs. a ghost that has
never gone down), and the haunting is specific (one ghost, one uptime
streak, one year) rather than a generic "beware" line. At 88 characters it
stays well inside the 120-character limit and reads cleanly aloud.
